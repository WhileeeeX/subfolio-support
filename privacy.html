隐私政策

适用范围

更新日期：2026年11月3日。Subfolio 由独立开发者 CHENG ZHONG 提供。本政策说明 App 和本支持网站如何处理信息。联系邮箱：applesandbox.zc@gmail.com。

本机数据

订阅、金额、商家、消费日期、付款方式、备注、头像、名字、签名及偏好设置用于提供订阅管理、记账、分析和提醒，默认保存在设备上。无需注册 Subfolio 账号。当前版本没有接入广告或第三方行为分析 SDK，也不出售个人信息。

照片、相机和识别

只有在你选择照片或使用拍照功能时处理相关图像。票据文字识别通过 Apple Vision 在设备上完成，不向开发者服务器或第三方 AI 服务发送票据图片。你确认保存后，识别出的文字作为流水记录保存。选作头像的照片会保存为个人资料的一部分。你可在系统设置中管理相机权限。

可选 iCloud 同步

开启同步后，订阅、流水、主要卡片选择及个人资料和部分偏好会通过 Apple CloudKit 存储到你的 iCloud 私有数据库，用于设备间同步。开发者不运营用于保存这些记录的自有服务器。关闭同步停止后续同步，但不会自动删除已有云端数据。Apple 按其隐私政策处理 iCloud 服务数据。

购买、提醒和汇率

购买通过 Apple App Store 处理，App 使用交易及权益状态判断 Pro 权限；开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息。提醒使用本地通知，需要你的授权。换算货币时访问 Frankfurter 汇率服务，请求包含币种代码，不包含你的账目、金额或备注；网络服务可能接收 IP 地址等必要连接信息。

保存、删除和导出

本地记录保留至你删除；会员到期不会删除已有记录。可在 App 内删除订阅和流水，并在个人设置中导出数据。开启同步时，记录删除会在成功同步后传递到其他设备。卸载 App 不等于删除 iCloud 或设备备份中的数据；云端及备份数据请通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 存储管理功能管理，或联系我们获取操作指导。自行导出的文件由你保管并删除。开发者无法代你直接读取或删除 iCloud 私有数据库。

邮件与本网站

联系支持时，我们会收到你的邮箱和自愿提供的内容，仅用于处理反馈与支持；不再需要时删除，法律要求保留的除外。你可以来信请求访问、更正或删除支持邮件中的个人信息。不要发送密码、验证码或完整支付凭证。本网站没有添加广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie；托管方 GitHub Pages 会为安全目的记录访问 IP 地址。

你的选择与政策更新

你可以关闭 iCloud 同步、撤销通知或相机权限，修改或删除记录。我们仅为所述功能和你的选择处理信息；必要时依据适用法律处理支持请求。服务商可能在不同地区处理服务数据，受其条款及适用法律约束。功能或处理方式变化时会更新本政策和日期；需要额外授权时会另行征求。

保存、刪除與匯出

郵件與本網站

聯絡支援時，我們收到你的信箱與自願提供的內容，僅用於回覆與支援；不再需要時刪除，法律要求保留者除外。可來信請求存取、更正或刪除支援郵件中的個人資訊。請勿寄送密碼、驗證碼或完整付款憑證。本站沒有廣告、分析腳本或追蹤 Cookie；GitHub Pages 會為安全目的記錄訪客 IP 位址。

Privacy Policy

Scope

Updated November 3, 2026. Subfolio is provided by independent developer CHENG ZHONG. This policy covers the app and this support website. Contact: applesandbox.zc@gmail.com.

On-device information

Subscriptions, amounts, merchants, dates, payment methods, notes, profile photos, names, bios and preferences support subscription management, expense tracking, analysis and reminders. They are stored on your device by default. No Subfolio account is required. The current app has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, and we do not sell personal information.

Photos, camera and recognition

Images are processed when you select a photo or use the camera. Receipt recognition uses Apple Vision on your device; receipt images are not sent to developer servers or third-party AI services. After you confirm and save, recognized text is stored as a transaction. A selected avatar is stored as profile information. Camera permissions can be managed in system settings.

Optional iCloud sync

When enabled, subscriptions, transactions, featured-card selections, profile information and some preferences are stored in your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit for cross-device sync. We do not operate our own server to store these records. Turning sync off stops subsequent syncing but does not delete existing cloud data. Apple processes iCloud service information under its privacy policy.

Purchases, reminders and exchange rates

Apple handles purchases. The app checks transaction and entitlement information to determine Pro access; we do not receive your payment-card details. Reminders use local notifications with your permission. Currency conversion requests to Frankfurter contain currency codes, not your records, amounts or notes. Network services may receive connection information such as your IP address.

Retention, deletion and export

Local records remain until you delete them; membership expiry does not delete them. Delete subscriptions and transactions in the app and export data in Personal Settings. With sync enabled, record deletions propagate after successful synchronization. Uninstalling does not delete iCloud data or device backups. Manage these using Apple’s iCloud storage controls or contact us for guidance. You control and delete your exported files. We cannot directly access or delete your private iCloud database.

Support email and this website

When you contact support, we receive your email address and information you choose to send, solely to handle feedback and support. We delete it when no longer needed unless legally required to retain it. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information in support correspondence. Do not send passwords, verification codes or full payment credentials. This website adds no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security.

Choices and updates

You can disable sync, revoke camera or notification permissions, and edit or delete records. We process information for the stated functions and your choices, and handle support requests under applicable law where necessary. Providers may process service data in different regions under their terms and applicable law. We update this policy and its date when practices change and request additional permission where required.
